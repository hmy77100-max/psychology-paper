# Routing principles

Interpret the user's ordinary language; do not require workflow commands. Identify the requested outcome before choosing a tool or skill.

Use one primary module per request:

- journal direction, fit, or target-journal style → `journal-fit-style`
- constructs, design, results, claim strength, or cross-section consistency → `evidence-audit`
- approved wording or translation candidate → `manuscript-writing`
- orchestration, approval recording, or unavailable-stage explanation → `psychology-paper`

Support modules receive only the current problem and a minimal handoff. They cannot widen scope.

Treat sentence scope as provisional. Spelling, grammar, punctuation, and meaning-preserving format remain local. Statistics, samples, exclusions, construct or operation names, study numbering, hypotheses, central findings, mechanism claims, and authoritative sources trigger a `change-impact audit` before writing.

Reading scope may expand only as needed to establish impact. Writing scope never expands without a separate user decision.
