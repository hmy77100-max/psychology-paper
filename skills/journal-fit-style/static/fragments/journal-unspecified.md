# Journal unspecified

Build the manuscript profile from the actual manuscript before naming journals. Identify the psychology branch, article and design types, central question, strongest evidence, evidence ceiling, contribution type, intended readers, largest limitation, and completion state.

Recommend three directions: ambitious, balanced, and safer. For each candidate, verify current scope and article type, perform only a light 1–2-article style scan, and state the trade-off. Ask which priority matters most: level, topical fit, speed, acceptance probability, cost, access model, or deadline. Do not choose for the user.
