# Confirmed journal

Treat the user-confirmed journal as authoritative. Verify its current official scope, article type, anonymization, ethics, data, length, reference, figure/table, supplement, and submission requirements. Then study comparable recent articles for organization and claim style.

Return high, medium, or low fit with reasons and the largest desk-review risk. If fit is low, offer the user choices—continue, reconsider, or make journal-neutral scientific revisions—without silently recommending or adopting another journal.
