# Journal analysis

Assess distinct dimensions rather than one similarity score:

1. Research question and audience.
2. Accepted article type and scope.
3. Contribution level and evidence expectations.
4. Current formal requirements and policies.
5. Observed organization and claim style in recent comparable articles.
6. Manuscript readiness and the largest submission risk.

Matching common vocabulary does not establish journal fit. Do not make a paper appear to be a different kind of study merely to imitate a journal. Separate live official requirements from article-derived observations in every output.
