# Full-review completion

Substance, not a checklist of headings:
- Contribution assessment: supported value, strengths and closest evidence.
- Section-level examination: applicable sections, figures/tables/supplements; located findings with evidence, impact and severity.
- Competing explanations: test author and reviewer reasoning; distinguish errors, gaps, limits and choices; explain outcomes of unresolved facts.
- Prioritized revision advice: essentials versus improvements; reporting, analysis or new research, benefits, costs and dependencies.

Follow entrypoint duties; disclose coverage. No minimum issue count. Deep review labels additions, corrections and unresolved findings; reuse IDs. Stop at feedback.
