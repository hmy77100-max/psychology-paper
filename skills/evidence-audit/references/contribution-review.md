# Contribution and target-journal comparison

This responsibility reviews the manuscript against existing context; it does not run journal positioning again. Reuse relevant article/profile fields, confirmed target, source locators and shared decisions already available. Without a target, review the supported contribution and state the limit on journal-specific assessment. Do not require profile creation to proceed.

## Compare evidence with the publication obstacle

For each material journal-related finding distinguish:

| Basis | What it can support |
|---|---|
| Official requirement | An explicit applicable requirement, with source, article type, scope and date checked |
| Publication example | An observed practice in the inspected examples, with selection and comparability limits |
| Model inference | A reasoned assessment of audience, contribution or likely reviewer concern, labeled as inference |

Use sufficient inspected sources already in context. If a stale or conflicting requirement could change advice, use targeted external verification to refresh that affected fact. Compare authority, currency and applicability; a newer example does not override an official rule. When current access is unavailable, retain uncertainty and continue independent review. Explain proposed corrections to shared context without silently overwriting it or reloading all profile materials.

Published examples are not universal thresholds. Two multi-study articles do not establish a minimum study count. An apparent journal mismatch is not itself a scientific defect. Separate scientific validity, contribution/explanation and journal/audience fit; an issue can affect more than one, with reasons. Do not convert inference into a rejection prediction or numerical acceptance probability.

## Preserve and assess the contribution

State what the inspected evidence adds for the intended audience and which supported strengths should survive revision. Judge contribution in relation to the question and comparable literature, not only sample size, significance or novelty language. Verify material literature-dependent claims through the existing targeted-evidence route; a limited search cannot establish first-ever novelty or absence of research.

For an alternative framing or journal recommendation, explain its supporting evidence, unresolved requirements and tradeoffs for the current argument. Keep the confirmed target/stance separate from recommendations. A literature conflict can justify discussion or a proposed change; it does not automatically adopt that change. Never conceal contrary/null results to improve apparent fit.

Deliver a compact connection: supported contribution -> obstacle -> basis and limits -> implication for the current target. Scale to requested scope; a local passage cannot establish whole-manuscript readiness. Prioritize material scientific/explicit requirement obstacles and dependencies before optional positioning polish, using model judgment and author resources. No mandatory score, ranking formula or second journal profile.
