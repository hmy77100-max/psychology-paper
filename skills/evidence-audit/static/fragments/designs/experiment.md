# Experimental design checks

Use when at least one factor is assigned or manipulated. Identify randomization, factors and levels, between/within structure, control condition, manipulation content, manipulation check, exclusions, outcomes, and planned model.

Randomization supports causal interpretation for the manipulated factor's effect under the implemented conditions. It does not make an unmanipulated mediator causal. Distinguish a manipulation check of the target state from compliance with instructions.
