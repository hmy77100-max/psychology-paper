# Instrument-based checks

Use for eye tracking, EEG, psychophysiology, neuroimaging, or other device-derived evidence. Identify device/model, acquisition settings, synchronization, preprocessing, artifact rejection, trial loss, feature derivation, analysis level, and quality thresholds.

Keep the chain explicit: physical signal → processed feature → statistical contrast → psychological interpretation. A significant device-derived feature does not uniquely identify a psychological construct without supporting validation.
