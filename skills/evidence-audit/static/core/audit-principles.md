# Audit principles

Use one of these evidence statuses:

- `ALIGNED`: claim and evidence match.
- `BOUNDED`: claim is supportable with an explicit scope or strength boundary.
- `CONDITIONAL`: interpretation depends on a stated condition, model, or result type.
- `UNRESOLVED`: available material cannot decide the issue.
- `CONTRADICTED`: visible evidence conflicts with the claim.
- `OUTSIDE_EVIDENCE`: the claim exceeds what the design or measure can establish.

Use risk levels R0–R4 from the shared risk contract. Separate the observed fact from the inference about its importance. Report the location, evidence status, risk, affected claim, and author question. Stop at feedback unless the user later approves a response.

Respect source authority. A historical thesis, current manuscript, raw data, and verification output are different sources even when they contain similar numbers. Do not recalculate without authorization and do not infer misconduct from inconsistency.
