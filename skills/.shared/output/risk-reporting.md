# Risk reporting

Use the requested threshold and report facts before recommendations.

- `R0`: integrity, ethics, consent, privacy, or mandatory compliance risk.
- `R1`: an issue reasonably capable of causing editorial screening rejection.
- `R2`: a central scientific issue likely to draw major-review criticism, major revision, or rejection.
- `R3`: an ordinary correction that improves accuracy, clarity, or reporting.
- `R4`: an optional refinement that does not materially affect validity or submission readiness.

For each reported issue, give the location, observed fact, why it matters at the selected threshold, and what remains unresolved. If the user requests only R0–R1 issues, omit R2–R4 rather than appending them as extra advice. Do not repeatedly relist an `INTENTIONAL_HOLD` during ordinary work; surface it only when the user asks for the relevant risk review or when it blocks the requested action.
