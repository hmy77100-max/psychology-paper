# Output contract

Use [the shared candidate format](../../../.shared/output/candidate-format.md). Keep the output copyable as TXT and preserve the required order for English editing, Chinese editing, or Chinese-to-English work.

Place author questions, data checks, and unresolved terminology after the candidate in a separate note section. Never place internal labels, risk codes, or workflow instructions inside manuscript prose.

Stage 1A returns candidates only. Do not modify, rebuild, rename, or duplicate manuscript files.
