# Evidence boundary

Treat `evidence_ceiling` as an input constraint. Preserve distinctions among observed pattern, association, prediction, randomized manipulation effect, moderation or conditional effect, mechanism interpretation, cross-study synthesis, and generalization.

Check apparent mismatches against available evidence before proposing a correction. Do not silently strengthen or weaken the author's claim. A material unresolved conflict warrants targeted `evidence-audit`; already verified changes within the author's scope need no automatic handoff. Unsupported claims cannot be presented as findings. An explanation may be discussed without pretending its mechanism was tested.
