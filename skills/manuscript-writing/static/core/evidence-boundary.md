# Evidence boundary

Treat `evidence_ceiling` as an input constraint. Preserve distinctions among observed pattern, association, prediction, randomized manipulation effect, moderation or conditional effect, mechanism interpretation, cross-study synthesis, and generalization.

If the requested wording exceeds the ceiling, do not silently soften or accept it. State the mismatch and route the claim to `evidence-audit`. Writing can clarify an approved boundary but cannot revise the underlying scientific decision.
