# Results

Use when writing results. Follow the analysis logic: data screening or checks, descriptive evidence, primary model, interaction decomposition, and planned secondary results as applicable. Preserve all statistics, signs, directions, degrees of freedom, intervals, and effect labels from the authoritative source.

Report the observed pattern before a brief result-level inference. Distinguish main effects, interactions, marginal effects, simple effects, and simple-simple effects. Leave broader theoretical explanation to the discussion.
