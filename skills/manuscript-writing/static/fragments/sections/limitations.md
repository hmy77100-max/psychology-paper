# Limitations

Use when defining evidentiary boundaries and future work. State the design or measurement feature, the inference it limits, and the concrete study needed next. Positive task-oriented phrasing is preferred, but direct disclosure of material limits must remain clear.

Do not convert a limitation into a confirmed boundary condition or claim that a future analysis has already solved it. Ethics, missing manipulation checks, uncertain scoring, and unavailable materials require factual disclosure rather than rhetorical reframing.
