# Keywords

In full-manuscript mode, derive keywords from the author-approved body and synchronized abstract, not from their position on page one. An explicit request to work only on keywords can override this sequence; record it without treating other units as approved.

Verify the journal's current keyword count, language and formatting requirements from its author instructions/profile. If unspecified, give a provisional candidate and mark only that requirement unresolved. Select searchable constructs, population/design when discriminating, and the article's supported contribution. Do not invent mechanisms, outcomes or causal claims to improve discoverability. Keep Chinese and English terminology aligned. Supply complete original keywords, candidate keywords and the applicable third part; let the author retain or replace any term.
